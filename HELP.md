# Roamy (Home Compass) — Help

Roamy is a compass-style navigation utility for iPhone (and Apple Watch). It is not turn-by-turn directions. Save a place, hold your phone up, and the dial points you back—with live distance.

There is **no account or login**. Home, Parked Car, and search targets stay on your device.

---

## Getting started

1. Allow **Location** when asked (needed for the map, distance, and sun/moon).
2. Open the **Compass** tab (default).
3. Set a **Home** or **Parked Car** location (see below).
4. Point the top of your phone toward the chevron on the dial—you’ll feel a haptic thump when you’re aligned.

**Tip:** Use a real iPhone outdoors. The Simulator can’t show true compass heading or a reliable GPS feel.

## Compass

### Reading the dial

- The bezel shows **N / E / S / W** and spins like a physical instrument.
- The map under the dial stays north-up relative to the world as you turn.
- Chevrons on the rim mark **Home**, **Parked Car**, and a search result.
- Distance pills under the dial show how far each target is. Tap a pill to peek at the map; expand for a full map sheet with range.

### Zoom

Use the frosted **+** and **−** buttons (designed for one-handed use). When you zoom in close enough, rim chevrons become pins on the map.

### Search

Use **Search places…** at the top to pick any place as a temporary target. Clear it later from the overflow menu (**Clear Search Result**).

### Nearby Areas (radar)

Toggle the radar control to show **Nearby Areas**—city/area nodes on distance rings.

- Needs a network connection; otherwise you may see **RADAR OFFLINE**.
- Tap a node to make it your active search target.

## Setting Home and Parked Car

### Home

1. Tap the Home control (if unset) or open the menu → **Set Home Location**.
2. In the **Set Home** sheet:
   - Search **“Search address or place…”**, or
   - Pan the map so the center crosshair sits on your spot, then tap **Set Home Here**, or
   - Tap **Use Current Location**.
3. You’ll see a confirmation when Home is saved.

Clear anytime via **Clear Home Location**.

### Parked Car

- With no car saved: tap the car control to quick-save your **current** GPS position.
- With a car saved: tap to show or hide it on the dial.
- **Long-press** (~½ second) to clear the parked car.
- Or use the menu: **Set Parked Car Location** / **Clear Parked Car Location**.

Saved targets sync to the Apple Watch app when the phone is connected.

## Sun Shadow

Explore how light falls at your location today.

- The porthole shows the ground map with sun/moon and cast shadows.
- Drag the time scrubber to “time travel” through **today**. Tap **NOW** (or **LIVE**) to return to the current time.
- Phase chips show lighting conditions such as **NIGHT**, **TWILIGHT**, **BLUE HOUR**, **GOLDEN HOUR**, **SOFT LIGHT**, and **HARD LIGHT**.
- Readouts include **AZIMUTH**, **ELEVATION**, **SUNRISE**, **SUNSET**, and moon visibility / altitude.
- **MAGIC HOURS** lists golden and blue windows (AM/PM). Tap a row to jump the scrubber.

If GPS isn’t ready yet, you’ll see **Acquiring GPS signal…**

## Location (Current Location)

Live place and motion data:

- **POSITION FIX** with **ACQUIRING** / **LIVE** status, plus a map preview (**EXPAND** → **Position Map**).
- **GEOGRAPHIC DATA**: area, city, street, district, state/province, country / postal.
- **RAW TELEMETRY**: latitude, longitude, course, GPS accuracy. Tap lat/lon to **copy** (you’ll see **COPIED**).
- Speed block: average speed, top speed, altitude (MSL), heading. Units follow your Settings (**km/h** or **mph**). Shows **STATIONARY** when you’re not moving.

## Data stamp photos

From **Compass** or **Location**, tap the camera control to capture a photo with a location data overlay.

1. Review **DATA STAMP PREVIEW**.
2. Optionally edit location / area name and add a **POLAROID NOTE**.
3. Share, or **Save to Photos**.

**Free:** a small number of complimentary stamped exports (then watch a short video, or upgrade to Pro).  
**Pro:** unlimited stamped exports, no ads.

Camera and Photos (Add Only) permissions are requested only when you use this feature.

## Apple Watch

The Watch app shows a dial focused on Home, Parked Car, or POI (use the Digital Crown to cycle focus).

From the Watch sheet you can:

- **Set Home Here** / **Clear Home**
- **Set Car Here** / **Clear Car**

Empty states may ask you to **Enable Location**, wait for GPS, or set Home/Car on the phone or Watch.

## Troubleshooting

**Compass feels off**  
Open **Settings → Calibrate Compass** and move the phone in a figure-8. Keep the phone away from metal mounts, magnets, and speakers.

**Stuck on Acquiring / no GPS**  
Go outdoors or near a window, wait a moment, and confirm Location is allowed for Roamy in iOS Settings.

**Radar shows RADAR OFFLINE**  
Check your network connection, then toggle Nearby Areas off and on.

**Wrong Home or car**  
Clear the target from the Compass menu and set it again. Changes are permanent until you re-set them.

**Watch not updating**  
Open the iPhone app nearby with Bluetooth on so Watch Connectivity can sync Home / Car / POI.

---

## What Roamy is (and isn’t)

- **Is:** a point-me-back compass with map, sun/moon light tools, telemetry, and optional photo stamps.
- **Isn’t:** a turn-by-turn navigation or multi-stop trip planner. One Home, one Parked Car, and one active search target at a time.
