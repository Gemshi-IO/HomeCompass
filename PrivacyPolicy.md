## Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how **Home Compass** (“**App**,” “**we**,” “**us**,” or “**our**”) handles information when you use our iOS application published by Gemshi (“**Developer**”).

Home Compass is a tactical navigation and telemetry tool. It uses on-device sensors and optional third-party services for analytics, advertising, and in-app purchases. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

This policy applies to the Home Compass mobile application. It does not apply to websites or services operated by third parties that may be linked from the App (for example, support or marketing pages), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

## Information We Collect and How We Use It

We design Home Compass to keep sensitive content on your device whenever possible. The sections below explain what data is involved, where it is processed, and why.

### Location Data

**What we use it for**

- Live GPS and related location information power core features, including compass headings, location telemetry, sun and shadow calculations, and map-related displays.

**How it is handled**

- Location is accessed **only when you use the App** and only to provide these features on your device.
- We **do not** track your location in the background for advertising or analytics purposes as part of our own systems.
- We **do not** maintain a history of your movements on our servers. Location data used for stamping or display is processed on your device in connection with the features you use.

**Your choices**

- You can enable or disable Location Services for Home Compass at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services** on your iPhone or iPad. Some features will not work without location permission.

### Camera, Photo Library, and Local Media

**What we use it for**

- The App lets you capture photos (with your permission), overlay live telemetry on those photos, and save or share the result.

**How it is handled — important**

- **All photo capture, stamping, rendering, saving, and sharing are performed locally on your device.**
- We **do not upload**, **do not collect**, and **do not store** your photos, camera captures, or stamped images on our servers.
- When you save to the Photos library or use the system share sheet, Apple’s and your chosen apps’ policies apply to what happens after you export content from Home Compass.

**Your choices**

- Camera and photo library access require your explicit permission through iOS. You can revoke access in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera** and **Photos**.

### Usage Data, Analytics, and Diagnostics

**What may be collected**

Through **Firebase Analytics** (Google), we may receive **aggregated and pseudonymous** usage information, such as:

- General app interaction events (for example, feature usage related to improving the product)
- Crash and stability-related signals
- Performance metrics

We use this information to understand how the App is used, fix bugs, and improve reliability and user experience. We do not use Firebase Analytics to build a profile of you for our own direct marketing.

**Advertising-related data**

Through **Google AdMob**, ads may involve collection of data by Google and its partners, such as:

- Device identifiers (including the **Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA)** if you have granted App Tracking Transparency permission, or limited alternatives when you have not)
- IP address and coarse location derived from IP
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions for rewarded ads)

Ads may be served as **non-personalized** or **personalized**, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law. We use advertising to support a free tier of the App; upgrading to **Pro** removes in-app ads as described in the App.

We do not sell your personal information as defined under applicable U.S. state privacy laws.

### Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

**What we use it for**

- A one-time **Pro** in-app purchase unlocks premium features (such as removing ads and unlimited stamped photo exports/sharing, as described in the App).

**How it is handled**

- All payment processing is handled by **Apple** through StoreKit. We **do not** receive or store your credit card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.
- We may receive confirmation of purchase entitlement (for example, that Pro is unlocked on your Apple ID) so the App can enable Pro features on your device.
